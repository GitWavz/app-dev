#  My Favorite Series: Attack on Titan
*Attack on Titan* is a dark fantasy action anime and manga series created by **Hajime Isayama**. 

Set in a post-apocalyptic world, humanity is forced to live inside three massive, concentric walls—**Wall Maria**, **Wall Rose**, and **Wall Sina**—to protect themselves from colossal, humanoid monsters known as **Titans**. The story begins when a giant Titan breaks through the outer wall, destroying protagonist **Eren Yeager**'s hometown and setting off a complex journey filled with mystery, political intrigue, military warfare, and intense combat.

---

## Main Characters

### 1. Eren Yeager
- **Role:** Main Protagonist
- **Affiliation:** Survey Corps (104th Training Corps)
- **Description:** Passionate, determined, and stubborn, Eren vows to eradicate every Titan from the world after witnessing the destruction of his home.

### 2. Mikasa Ackerman
- **Role:** Main Protagonist / Top Recruit
- **Affiliation:** Survey Corps (104th Training Corps)
- **Description:** Eren's adoptive sister and an exceptionally skilled warrior with unmatched combat instincts, deeply devoted to keeping Eren safe.

### 3. Armin Arlert
- **Role:** Main Protagonist / Strategist
- **Affiliation:** Survey Corps (104th Training Corps)
- **Description:** Eren and Mikasa's childhood friend. Though physically weaker than his peers, Armin is a brilliant strategist whose tactical mind proves vital to humanity's survival.

### 4. Levi Ackerman
- **Role:** Captain of the Special Operations Squad
- **Affiliation:** Survey Corps
- **Description:** Widely recognized as **"Humanity's Strongest Soldier."** Levi is clean-obsessed, blunt, and extremely formidable in combat against Titans.

### 5. Erwin Smith
- **Role:** 13th Commander
- **Affiliation:** Survey Corps
- **Description:** A ruthless, charismatic, and visionary leader willing to make sacrifices to uncover the truth of the world beyond the walls.
